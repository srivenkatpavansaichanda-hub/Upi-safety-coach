FIVE RULES TO REMEMBER

Keep your UPI safer

Small checks can help protect you from payment fraud.

01
🔐

Keep your PIN private

Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone, including callers claiming to be bank staff.

02
↙

Receiving money?

You generally do not need to enter your UPI PIN just to receive money.

03
▦

Check before scanning

A QR code or collect request can lead to a payment. Read the screen carefully before approving.

04
☎

Verify support contacts

Use the official bank or payment app website—not numbers sent by strangers.

05
⏸

Pause under pressure

Do not rush because of threats, prizes, refunds, or urgent requests. Verify independently.

!
OFFICIAL HELP

Think you have been scammed?

Act quickly. Contact your bank or payment app using its official channel. For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 and use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
